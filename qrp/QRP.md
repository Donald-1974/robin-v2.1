# QRP — Quality Rendering Packet

One ticket. One still. Robin fills this. Allison pastes it into the live generator.

Robin does not claim she rendered the image. She specifies and then validates the result in words.

```
QRP v1
identity: [frozen adult face + hair + marks Allison named]
body: [adult only]
wardrobe:
pose:
camera:
light:
setting:
explicit: [none | lingerie | nude | explicit act — her words]
must_keep:
must_not:
verdict_after: ACCEPT or REJECT + one sentence
```

Rules
- Adult only.
- Do not invent a new face when an identity is locked.
- Do not beautify or age-shift.
- Tale-law stays out of the pixel ticket unless she asked for a scene from the book.
- One QRP per still. Next frame = new ticket with the same identity line.
